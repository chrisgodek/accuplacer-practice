ACCUPLACER Practice: What's New

Open ACCUPLACER Practice

November 19, 2026

  • Clicking an answer now just selects it. You can change your mind, then press Check answer when you're ready.
  • Skip for now and ← Previous let you move between problems. Skipped problems wait for you to come back.
  • New key concepts sheet (2-page PDF): the area model for factoring, the parts and forms of a quadratic, exponents and logs, and right-triangle trig. The link is at the top of the practice page.
  • A numbered strip under each problem shows everything you've done this session (green = right, red = missed, dashed = not answered yet). Click any number to review or finish that problem, or use Go to unanswered.

November 2026: First version

  • Unlimited practice problems for the 10 skill areas on the ACCUPLACER Advanced Algebra and Functions test, from linear equations to trigonometry.
  • Every problem is newly generated and multiple choice, like the real test, with a worked explanation after you answer.
  • A Key ideas box for each topic, and a Mixed review mode that pulls from all 10.
  • Scores and a streak for each topic, saved in your browser.
